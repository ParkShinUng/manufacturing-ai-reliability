## Quality flags (closed enum)

Data-quality flags are a separate closed vocabulary carried on telemetry events. See
`docs/02-architecture/TIME_AND_DATA_QUALITY.md` §5 and
`contracts/jsonschema/v1/telemetry.schema.json`, which is authoritative:

`SENSOR_MISSING`, `SENSOR_DISCONNECTED`, `SENSOR_FROZEN`, `VALUE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `VALUE_NOT_FINITE`,
`STALE_READING`, `OUTLIER_SUSPECTED`, `TIMESTAMP_REVERSED`, `TIMESTAMP_SYNTHESISED`,
`DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED`, `SEQUENCE_GAP`, `BUFFER_OVERFLOW_DROP`.

## Removed in v0.3

- `RATE_CHANGE_LIMITED` — ambiguous between clamp and reject. Split into `RATE_CHANGE_CLAMPED`
  (non-rejecting modifier on an ACCEPT) and `RATE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` (rejecting). See
  `AI_SAFETY_AND_MLOPS.md` §2.2.
